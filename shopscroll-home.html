Design choices

  • Order follows intent. Cart first (what you're about to buy), then budget (what it costs you), then discovery (what you might add).
  • Cart only appears with items. Use "Demo: empty cart" to see the page without it; the budget section moves to the top.
  • Budget is a horizontal bar chart. A large bar for the month, then one bar per category. Solid plum is in your cart, hatched is wishlist, and a red outline with an "over by" note appears when a category is over budget.
  • Everything is connected. Change quantities or remove items and the chart updates. Heart or add a find and it shows up in the chart straight away.
  • Toggle "Include wishlist" to compare firm spending with the optimistic total.
  • Today's finds keeps the existing card and action bar. Drag the card sideways or press the heart to save it, and the X to skip.
  • The right column keeps taste and friends, and the old wishlist budget card moved into the chart.
