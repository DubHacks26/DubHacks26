Design choices

The feed copies the Reels frame: one tall card, centred, with everything else pushed to the edges so the product is the only thing on screen.

Paging

  • One item at a time. A scroll, flick, or swipe moves exactly one item with a short eased slide. Further wheel momentum is ignored until the slide finishes, so a hard flick never skips items.
  • On touch, the card follows your finger while you drag up or down and commits past a short threshold or a quick flick.
  • Up and down buttons at the right edge, plus the arrow keys, do the same.

Hearting

  • Drag the card left or right; past 90px the item is hearted. The direction is locked after the first few pixels, so a vertical page never accidentally hearts.
  • Also: double-click, the heart in the action rail, or the H key.

Layout

  • Action rail beside the card: heart with count, comments, share, save, more, and the brand thumbnail.
  • On wide screens the brand, Follow, and tags sit in the left gutter. Narrower screens move the brand inside the card, and phones put the rail over the card.
  • On the card: category, product name, price, one-line pitch, rating, and details link.

Colour and type

NightPlumHeartLilac

Dark surroundings make the product colours glow. Pink is only used for hearts. Instrument Serif for product names, DM Sans for everything functional.

Swapping in photos

Replace each .art block with a full-bleed <img> using object-fit:cover. Video works the same way.
